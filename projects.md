    <details class="entry-card">
      <summary>
        <span>
          <span class="entry-title">101Lease · Civil Machines Technologies</span>
          <span class="entry-meta">Co-founder / Founding Engineer-level initiative · Tech Lead</span>
        </span>
      </summary>
      <div class="entry-body">
        <p>The public website is no longer active. This case study records a founding-engineer-level product initiative and its substantial engineering and business outcomes.</p>
        <ul>
          <li>Developed an end-to-end asset-management product using a modular architecture, enabling customization with minimal engineering effort for new deployments.</li>
          <li>Implemented lead management, company hierarchy, asset management, rental collection, client and broker aggregation, deal aggregation, and an analytics dashboard for location insights.</li>
          <li>Created four successful spin-offs from the base product, including one customized for the hospitality industry.</li>
          <li>Reduced the lead-management team from 120 to 20, an <strong>80% reduction</strong>, within 30 days of deployment.</li>
          <li>Increased SafeHoms revenue by <strong>125%</strong>, from 80 Lakhs to 1.8 Crores per month, within 60 days of deployment.</li>
          <li>Delivered a <strong>120% revenue increase</strong> in the first quarter of another deployment.</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </details>

          <span class="entry-meta">Product Team Lead · Hyperlocal bakery marketplace</span>

      </summary>
      <div class="entry-body">
        <p>Sweet2th is a hyperlocal marketplace for the Indian bakery industry, connecting consumers, bakers, vendors, and delivery partners.</p>
        <ul>
          <li>Led an eight-person engineering team through a one-year product-development cycle.</li>
          <li>Designed and implemented a generic system representing all marketplace relationships with configurable controls and dynamic payout and commission structures.</li>
          <li>Developed a multi-platform solution with a Django backend, Flutter mobile applications for vendors, consumers, and delivery partners, and React web applications for vendors, consumers, and a live management portal.</li>
          <li>Engineered configurable marketplace controls and dynamic payouts and commissions based on grievances, ratings, delivery timing, and orders.</li>
          <li>Built grievance handling and delivery-performance monitoring.</li>
        </ul>
