## What had to scale

I helped grow Certa's remote engineering team from 5 to 50 through Series B. I researched ways to structure the team and chose a Spotify-inspired model because it fit how we wanted teams to own their work. We experimented with it in a remote-first environment and adapted it as we grew.

## What made it work remotely

Autonomy calls for proactive communication. I wanted people to surface risks early and give teammates enough context to keep moving across time zones. A structured thread should make the question, decision, and owner easy to find. That lets someone catch up and act without attending every conversation.

For me, servant leadership meant making ownership clear and helping teams get the context they needed to make decisions. The aim was for teams to make routine decisions without routing them through me.
