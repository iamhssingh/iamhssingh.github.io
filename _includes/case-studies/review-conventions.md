## The conflict

Two senior engineers were stretching out a PR over variable naming and conventions. Each had a reasonable position. There was no team standard that a reviewer could point to, so the PR had become the place to decide one.

## What I changed

I asked them to bring the proposed convention to the team. Proposing a convention means explaining why it helps, showing examples, and hearing objections. If the team agrees, we document it in a place everyone can find. That shared decision is what a reviewer can cite on the next PR.

For style and naming, an undocumented preference is a suggestion. A documented agreement can be a requirement. I see this as servant leadership: give people a clear way to resolve the disagreement and let the team own the standard.
