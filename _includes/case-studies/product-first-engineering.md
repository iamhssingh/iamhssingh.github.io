## I start with what the product needs to learn

I usually lean toward product when a team is weighing a perfect architecture against the next thing users need. If a small version can answer whether the workflow is useful, I want to put that version in front of people quickly. Usage gives us a basis for deciding what to build or scale next.

I still make deliberate choices about data, access, and interfaces when a mistake there would be expensive to reverse. I leave more room to change internal structure or infrastructure that depends on demand we have yet to see.

[Data AI @ Certa](../certa-data-ai/) is an example. Vendors were spending months filling in RFPs. We prefilled answers from documents they already had, and I made citations part of the first design so vendors could verify each answer before submitting. The product metric was submission time. It fell from one to three months to under two weeks. That is the kind of result I want to see before investing in more automation.
