## Turn vendor form entry into evidence review

Vendors were spending one to three months completing submissions. Much of the requested information already existed in pitch decks, PDFs, and public sources. I built Data AI with an ML engineer in 2024 so vendors could spend their time verifying answers and filling gaps.

## Put the evidence in the form

I made source attribution a product requirement. The system drafted answers from source material and prefilled each RFP field with a citation. Vendors could inspect the source beside the answer where they already worked. That made verification part of the submission flow.

## Measure the whole workflow

Vendor submission time fell from one to three months to under two weeks, including human review.

Certa’s [Data Agent announcement](https://www.certa.ai/resources/introducing-os3-and-a-next-generation-of-ai-agents) describes prefilling from policies, documents, and web sources with linked evidence.
