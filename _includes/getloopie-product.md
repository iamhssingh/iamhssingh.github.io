## The product problem

A commitment can start in a meeting, gain context in chat, and end up in a ticket. GetLoopie.ai brought that context together so teams could follow through in Slack and the work tools they already used. My co-founder Mahen led most of the AI work. I focused on product engineering, backend architecture, integrations, deployment, and validation through five pilots.

## Product point of view

Companies build custom workflows around the tools, terminology, and routines their teams already use. The same incident can call for an engineering response, a product update, or a follow-up in a ticket. Our bet was that an agent could handle those recurring engineering handoffs in Slack and the connected work tools.

[Viktor launched in Slack in March 2026](https://viktor.com/about), and Anthropic [introduced Claude Tag in June 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/news/introducing-claude-tag). Both are useful comparisons for agents that meet teams where they work. GetLoopie focused on engineering follow-through: which events needed processing, which evidence could be used, and where the follow-up belonged.
